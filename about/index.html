About Kyro

Kyro is a selected resource hub and tool directory built for After Effects editors and motion designers. Established in November 2026, the platform provides free, verified extensions, scripts, presets, project files, and editing assets in one fast, clutter-free index.

Why Discord Verification?

Downloads are gated through the Kyro Discord server to help grow the community, keep links clean, and connect editors directly with support, installation guides, and live update drops.

Safety & Antivirus Notice

All software and scripts are tested prior to release, though we always recommend scanning files with VirusTotal before installing. Unsigned .jsxbin scripts and .zxp CEP extensions occasionally trigger false positives in Windows Defender or third-party antivirus tools—this is normal behavior for custom After Effects extensions.

Support & Troubleshooting

Need help with an install, plugin error, or broken link? Do not DM the owner directly—head over to the #help channel in the official Kyro Discord server so staff and the community can assist you faster.

Support Kyro

Everything on Kyro is free to use. If the site has saved you time or leveled up your edits, consider donating—every single donation goes straight toward funding new resources, tools, and packs for the vault (and directly covers server costs and funds new tools for the vault).

DMCA & Copyright Policy

Kyro acts strictly as a community directory/index. Copyrighted software binaries are not hosted directly on Kyro's web server; download links point to external file hosts. All copyrights and trademarks remain with their respective rights holders.

Kyro responds promptly to valid takedown notices and removes identified links after review. Rights holders requesting immediate link removal can contact the moderation team in the Kyro Discord and ask for a copyright-removal ticket.

Include the copyrighted work, the exact Kyro page and download link, your contact details, confirmation that you are the rights holder or their authorized representative, and a statement that the notice is accurate and submitted in good faith.

Disclaimer

Kyro is an independent, community-driven resource directory and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Adobe Inc. All trademarks, software names, and plugins belong to their respective creators.
